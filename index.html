Ways of Working

Skills I bring to the table

Research
Understanding people first
I lead with empathy, listening before solving. My research practice is built on genuinely wanting to understand the person on the other side of the screen.
  • User interviews and contextual inquiry
  • Ethnographic research and field observation
  • Usability testing, moderated and unmoderated
  • Affinity mapping and thematic analysis
  • Journey mapping and user personas
UX / UI Design
Craft that communicates and converts
I design interfaces that are not just visually sharp but behaviourally sound, grounded in how people actually think and interact with digital products.
  • Wireframing, hi-fidelity mockups and design systems
  • Heuristic evaluation and cognitive walkthrough
  • Interaction design and micro-interaction thinking
  • WCAG 2.2 accessibility standards
  • Cross-platform design, iOS, Android, and Web
Prototyping
Test before you build
From rough wireframes to high-fidelity interactive prototypes, I get ideas in front of real users fast to validate direction and reduce risk before development begins.
  • Interactive prototyping in Figma and Framer
  • High-complexity flows with Axure RP
  • Rapid iteration based on user feedback
  • Prototype-led user testing via Maze
Design with Business Goals
Where design meets outcomes
Great design is not just good UX, it moves metrics. I connect every design decision to business objectives, making it easy to prioritise what actually creates value.
  • UX metrics and KPI alignment
  • Data analysis with Google Analytics, Power BI, and Looker
  • Competitive analysis and UX audits
  • Double Diamond and Design Thinking frameworks
Systems Thinking
Design that scales
I build component libraries, token systems, and IA frameworks that keep products consistent as they grow, making design and engineering faster together.
  • Design systems and component architecture
  • Information architecture and navigation design
  • Figma variables and token management
Collaboration
Cross-team alignment
I thrive in cross-functional teams, bridging product, engineering, and stakeholders through clear communication, structured workshops, and shared design language.
  • Design sprints and ideation workshops
  • Agile development collaboration
  • Stakeholder presentations and design critique

How I Work

My design process

1
Discover
User interviews, ethnographic research, competitive analysis, and heuristic evaluation to understand the real problem space.
2
Define
Synthesising research into personas, journey maps, and clear problem statements. Double Diamond thinking to narrow what to solve.
3
Design
Wireframes, hi-fi UI, and interactive prototypes, tested and iterated with users via Maze and moderated sessions.
4
Deliver
Dev-ready specs, design system handoff, QA of UI, and post-launch measurement with analytics to validate outcomes.
