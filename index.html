How I Work

My design process

1
Discover
User interviews, ethnographic research, competitive analysis, and heuristic evaluation to understand the real problem space.
2
Define
Synthesising research into personas, journey maps, and clear problem statements. Double Diamond thinking to narrow what to solve.
3
Design
Wireframes, hi-fi UI, and interactive prototypes, tested and iterated with users via Maze and moderated sessions.
4
Deliver
Dev-ready specs, design system handoff, QA of UI, and post-launch measurement with analytics to validate outcomes.
